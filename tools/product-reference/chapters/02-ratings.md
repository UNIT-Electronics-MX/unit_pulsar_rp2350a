## **2 Ratings**

This chapter defines the recommended operating conditions and electrical characteristics of the UNIT PULSAR RP2350A hardware V1.3.0. Ratings apply to the complete board unless otherwise stated.

### **2.1 Recommended Operating Conditions** {.section-page}

The UNIT PULSAR RP2350A can be powered from USB-C, VIN, or a single-cell LiPo battery. The onboard AP2112K regulator supplies the 3.3 V logic domain used by the RP2350A and onboard peripherals.

| Parameter | Recommended operating condition | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| USB-C input | 5 V nominal | Primary power and programming interface |
| VIN input | Up to 6 V | External board supply input |
| Battery input | 3.7 V nominal, 4.2 V maximum | Single-cell LiPo battery |
| Regulated logic rail | 3.3 V nominal | Generated by the onboard AP2112K-3.3 regulator |
| 3.3 V regulator output capability | Up to 600 mA | Shared by the RP2350A, onboard peripherals, LEDs, and external 3.3 V loads |
| Battery charge current | 200 mA nominal | Configured by the MCP73831 PROG resistor |
| RP2350 system clock | Up to 150 MHz | Firmware configurable |
| Board reference oscillator | 12 MHz | Onboard reference oscillator |
| Internal SRAM | 520 kB | RP2350A internal memory |
| External flash | 128 Mbit / 16 MiB | W25Q128JVPIQ |
| External PSRAM | 8 MiB | APS6404L-3SQR-ZR |
| Logic level | 3.3 V | Applies to onboard digital interfaces and GPIO |

### **2.2 Power Domains and Power Distribution**

The UNIT PULSAR RP2350A uses separate power domains for external supply, battery operation, system power, and regulated 3.3 V logic.

The main power domains are:

- **USB-C / VBUS:** provides the nominal 5 V USB supply used for board power, programming, and battery charging.

- **VIN:** external supply input supporting up to 6 V.

- **VBAT:** battery domain intended for a single-cell LiPo battery with a nominal voltage of 3.7 V and a maximum fully charged voltage of 4.2 V. The battery input includes reverse-polarity protection.

- **VSYS:** internal system supply used as the input to the 3.3 V regulator. The board power-selection circuitry routes the available supply source to this domain.

- **3.3 V:** regulated logic supply generated by the AP2112K-3.3. This rail powers the RP2350A, external flash, PSRAM, BMI270, PDM microphone, microSD interface, QWIIC interface, RGB LEDs, and other onboard 3.3 V circuitry.

The AP2112K-3.3 regulator supports up to 600 mA output current. This current is shared between the RP2350A, onboard peripherals, status and RGB LEDs, and any external device powered from the 3.3 V rail. The current available to external loads therefore depends on the active board configuration and application workload.

The MCP73831T-2ACI/OT manages single-cell LiPo battery charging. Hardware V1.3.0 is configured for a nominal charge current of 200 mA. The charging current is determined by the PROG resistor and may be changed by modifying its value according to the MCP73831 configuration requirements.

All onboard digital interfaces operate in the 3.3 V logic domain. QWIIC, microSD, HSTX, and exposed GPIO signals must therefore be used with 3.3 V-compatible devices unless external level translation is provided.

Connector mechanical current ratings must not be interpreted as board power ratings. Maximum usable current is also constrained by the associated PCB traces, power path, regulator, protection components, and operating conditions.

### **2.3 Digital Interfaces**

All onboard digital peripherals are connected to the board's 3.3 V logic
domain. External logic compatibility must nevertheless be checked against the
RP2350A input/output specifications and the power state of both devices.

| Interface | Board connection | Electrical note |
|---|---|---|
| Internal I2C | GPIO8 / GPIO9 | BMI270 bus with onboard pull-ups |
| QWIIC I2C | GPIO24 / GPIO25 | Connector also supplies 3.3 V and GND |
| microSD | GPIO2–GPIO7 | Full SDIO group; wiki uses SPI-compatible subset |
| PDM | GPIO10 / GPIO11 | Clock output and microphone data input |
| HSTX | GPIO12–GPIO19 | High-speed driven pairs; reserve while video is active |
| WS2812 data | GPIO1 | Drives three cascaded onboard LEDs |
| SWD | SWDIO / SWCLK | Use a 3.3 V target reference |

### **2.5 Unspecified Board Characteristics** {.section-page}

- `VIN`, `VBAT`, and USB operating and absolute-maximum ranges
- Available 3.3 V current for external loads
- Battery charge current and supported cell/cable compatibility
- Source priority and reverse-current behavior among USB, `VIN`, and battery
- Total current consumption in boot, idle, storage, video, and audio modes
- GPIO drive and input thresholds as exposed by each connector
- Validated QSPI, PSRAM, SDIO, PDM, I2C, and HSTX operating rates
- Board-level temperature, humidity, ESD, and thermal performance

### **2.6 Electrical Precautions** {.section-page}

1. Begin first power-up from a current-limited USB source.
2. Connect ground before external clocks, data, or analog signals.
3. Do not connect a battery without documented polarity, chemistry, voltage,
   connector, and charge-current compatibility.
4. Do not power the 3.3 V rail simultaneously from the board regulator and an
   external source unless that operating mode is explicitly approved.
5. Do not share GPIO12–GPIO19 with other driven circuits while HSTX video is
   active.
6. Remove power before changing FFC/FPC, microSD, or battery connections.
